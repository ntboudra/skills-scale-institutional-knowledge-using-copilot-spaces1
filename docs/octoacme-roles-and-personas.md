# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality assurance strategy, establish testing standards, and ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release. They work with developers and product managers to define testability requirements and validation approaches.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test strategy and quality standards for the project
- Create and prioritize test plans aligned with release timelines
- Define acceptance testing criteria in collaboration with Product Manager
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Participate in release readiness reviews
- Mentor developers on testing best practices

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal post-launch defects
- Reduce testing cycle time through automation and efficient prioritization
- Build quality into the development process, not just at the end

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning (testing scope and capacity)
- Daily standups (blockers, test coverage status)
- Release readiness review
- Weekly sync with PM on quality metrics

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers:** Collaborate on testability design, automated test coverage, and defect triage
- **With Product Manager:** Align on acceptance criteria and user scenarios to test
- **With Project Manager:** Coordinate QA phases in project timeline and escalate quality risks

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic direction, and funding/approval decisions for projects. They represent customer needs, business value, and organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Approve project scope and budget
- Participate in project kickoff and gate reviews
- Provide feedback on prioritization and trade-offs
- Escalate resource or strategic blockers
- Participate in release announcements and customer communications

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between delivery and business strategy
- Reduce project surprises through proactive communication

### Typical Communication
- Kickoff meeting (context, constraints, success metrics)
- Monthly stakeholder updates (progress, risks, approvals needed)
- Gate reviews at major milestones
- Ad-hoc escalation calls for critical decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Manager:** Receive and approve project updates, escalation of risks/scope changes
- **With Product Manager:** Validate business outcomes and prioritization decisions
- **With Developers:** Attend demos and provide feedback on delivered features

---

## Technical Architect / Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects and Tech Leads guide the overall technical direction, design decisions, and engineering standards. They identify architectural risks, propose technical solutions, and ensure systems are scalable and maintainable.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns for the project
- Review technical proposals and validate feasibility
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and debt
- Establish coding standards and engineering practices
- Mentor developers on best practices and design patterns
- Participate in design reviews and major code decisions
- Guide performance, security, and scalability requirements

### Goals
- Build systems that are scalable, maintainable, and secure
- Reduce technical debt and accelerate future feature velocity
- Mentor the team to raise engineering discipline

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning (technical estimation and feasibility)
- Design reviews (async or synchronous)
- Technical spike discussions
- Weekly sync with PM on technical blockers or trade-offs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers:** Collaborate on design, code reviews, and technical problem-solving
- **With Product Manager:** Discuss trade-offs between scope and technical feasibility
- **With Project Manager:** Identify and communicate technical dependencies and risks

---

## Design / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design and UX Leads own the user experience strategy, design systems, and usability validation. They ensure features are intuitive, consistent, and meet user needs before engineering effort is expended.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain design systems and component libraries
- Design user workflows and interface mockups
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Define accessibility and interaction standards
- Participate in feature refinement and acceptance criteria definition
- Review implemented features for design fidelity and usability
- Establish design critique and feedback processes

### Goals
- Deliver user experiences that are intuitive and delightful
- Reduce rework by validating designs before development
- Maintain consistency across products through design systems

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning (design readiness and UX scope)
- Design critiques and feedback sessions
- User research findings and insights
- Weekly sync with Product Manager on design trade-offs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Product Manager:** Validate feature concepts and user scenarios
- **With Developers:** Collaborate on implementation details and feasibility
- **With Project Manager:** Communicate design dependencies and timeline impact
